Help

Support

Stuck, found a bug, or have an idea? Email us at support@plandit.app. Tell us what you were doing and, if you can, add a screenshot.

Save a TikTok or Instagram post

In TikTok or Instagram, tap Share on the post, then choose Plandit. If you don't see Plandit, tap More and turn it on. You can also copy the post's link and paste it in Plandit. When Plandit isn't sure which place a post is about, it asks you to pick.

Plans, prices and hours

Prices, opening hours and travel times are estimates. You can change a stop's price or time in your plan. Check that a place is open before you go.

Forgot your password

If you use Sign in with Apple, there's no Plandit password to forget. If you signed up with email, write to us from that address and we'll help you get back in.

Delete your account

In the app, go to Settings → Account → Delete account. This deletes your account and everything in it. If you can't get into the app, email us from your account's address and we'll delete it for you. Our Privacy Policy explains what we keep and for how long.

Is Plandit free?

Yes. There's nothing to buy in the app.
