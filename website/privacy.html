Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

This policy explains what Plandit, the iPhone app and this website, collects, why, who else sees it, and how to delete it. The short version: we keep what we need to run your account and your plans, we don't sell it, we don't show ads, and we don't track you across other apps or websites. Our Terms of Service cover the rest of the rules for using Plandit.

1. Who we are

Plandit is built by a small student team. "Plandit", "we" and "us" mean that team. You can reach us at support@plandit.app.

2. What we collect

  • Your account. Your email address, and your name if you give it. If you use Sign in with Apple, Apple gives us your email (which can be a private relay address Apple creates for you) and, the first time, your name. If you sign up with email, your password goes to our account provider, which stores it in a scrambled (hashed) form. Plandit's own server never sees it.
  • Your profile. What you fill in: your name, school, home base (whatever you type, such as a neighborhood), default budget and how you like to get around.
  • What you save and plan. The links you share to Plandit and their captions, the creator's handle and preview image link, the places you confirm (name, address, hours and map position of the place), the prices you set, your plans, the stops and times in them, and the places you've marked as visited. This is synced to your account so it's there when you sign in on another phone.
  • How you use Plandit. When you save a link, save a place or build a plan, and which days you open the app. We record these with your account so we can count active users and see what's working.
  • Technical logs. Like most online services, our servers and hosting providers log requests, including IP addresses and times, to keep Plandit running and secure. We don't store IP addresses in our database.
  • The waitlist. If you join the waitlist on this website, we keep the email you enter.

3. What stays on your phone

  • Your location. If you allow it, Plandit uses your location only on your phone, to show where you are on the map. It isn't sent to us. You can turn it off any time in your iPhone's Settings.
  • Videos you attach. If you attach a video to help find a place, Plandit reads the on-screen text and speech on your phone, then deletes its copy. The video and its audio are never uploaded to us. Only the text it finds is used to search Apple Maps for the place.
  • Your sign-in. Your sign-in session is kept in your iPhone's Keychain.

4. Imports from TikTok and Instagram

When you share a TikTok or Instagram post to Plandit, the app sends the post's link to our server. Our server reads the post's public page to get its caption, which helps work out which place it's about. We don't send TikTok or Instagram anything about you. If our server can't help, the app may ask TikTok for the post's public details directly, and TikTok will see that request come from your phone. The caption is kept with your saved place.

5. How we use it

  • To run Plandit: sign you in, find places, build and sync your plans.
  • To understand and improve Plandit, using counts like how many people signed up, built a plan or came back this week.
  • To keep Plandit secure and stop abuse, such as enforcing daily import limits.
  • To contact you about your account, or, if you joined the waitlist, to send one email when Plandit is ready.

6. Who else sees it

We share information only with services that run Plandit for us, and only what each one needs:

  • Supabase runs our accounts and database.
  • Render runs our server.
  • Vercel hosts this website. The website also loads its font from Google Fonts, so Google sees your IP address when the page loads.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple and Apple Maps. Place searches and routes between your stops go to Apple Maps.
  • TikTok and Instagram receive requests for public post pages, as described above.

Plandit started as a class project, and we report totals such as the number of sign-ups and active users to our course staff. Those reports never include your name, email or what you saved.

We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect people's safety, or if Plandit is ever transferred to someone new, in which case this policy keeps applying to your information.

7. What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your information.
  • We don't show ads, and we don't use advertising or analytics trackers in the app or on this website.
  • We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

8. How long we keep it

  • Your account, profile, saved places, plans and usage records stay until you delete your account.
  • When you delete your account, we delete it and everything in it right away. Copies can stay in our providers' backups for a short time before they're overwritten. We keep a record that the account ID was deleted, with no name or email, so it can't be brought back by mistake.
  • Server logs are kept for the time our hosting providers keep them.
  • Waitlist emails stay until you ask us to remove yours.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Change your profile any time in the app.
  • Delete your account and everything in it in the app: Settings → Account → Delete account.
  • Turn off location in your iPhone's Settings. Plandit still works without it.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, to correct it, or to delete it, including a waitlist email, by writing to support@plandit.app. Depending on where you live, you may have other rights over your information, and we'll honor them.

10. Children

Plandit isn't for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you think a child under 13 has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

11. Security and where it's stored

Information travels to and from Plandit over encrypted connections, and access to our database is restricted. No service can promise perfect security, but we work to protect what you trust us with. Our servers and database are in the United States.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Plandit changes. We'll post the new version here with a new effective date. If a change is significant, we'll also tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.

13. Contact us

Questions about your privacy? Email us at support@plandit.app.
