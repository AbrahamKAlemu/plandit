## What's New

> • Send your plan as a link. Friends open it in their browser, no app or account needed, and tap
> "I'm in" or "Can't make it".
> • Plan with friends: send an "Ask friends what works" link, and Plandit builds one plan that fits
> the group's budget and free time. Friends with Plandit can join your plan and change it with you.
> • Plans from your saves: once you've saved a couple of places, a day with no plan yet shows
> ready-made plans built from them.
> • Change your plan on the go: reorder or remove stops, change a stop's time, and undo any change.
> Mark each stop Planned, I'm here or Done, and move the rest of your day later in one tap when
> you're running behind.
> • Share a TikTok, a Reel or a video from Photos to Plandit, and it finds the place and adds it to
> Saved, asking you when it isn't sure.
> • A simpler bottom bar: Today, Saved, Plan and Profile. Your settings now live in Profile.
> • Saved has category tiles, Filter and Sort, so the place you want is quicker to find.
> • A quick tour you can skip the first time you open Plandit, and a new icon.

**Notes** (3,731 of 4,000 characters):

> 1. SCREEN RECORDING
> Attached (Attachment). Recorded on a physical iPhone with this build, starting at launch. It
> shows: creating an account with Sign in with Apple, saving a place from a TikTok link, deleting
> that account (Profile > Account > Delete account, which asks Apple to confirm), signing in to the
> demo account, planning a day, the plan on the map, changing how to get between two stops, and
> sharing the plan as a link, then stopping sharing.
> Nothing in the app is paid.
>
> 2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
> Plandit is a free day planner for college students in New York City. Students save TikToks and
> Reels of places they want to go, then rarely turn them into an actual day out. Plandit finds the
> place a shared post names and keeps it on a map. When the student has free time, it builds a
> plan for today from the places they pick: the order of stops, the time at each, how to get
> between them (walk, subway, bus or drive) and the cost per person against their budget.
>
> 3. HOW TO USE IT
> - Sign in with the demo account in Sign-In Information above ("Continue with email", then "Sign
> in"). You can also create an account with email (no email confirmation) or Sign in with Apple.
> A short tour shows on first sign-in; tap Skip to close it.
> - The demo account already has saved places. To plan a day: tap Plan (+) in the bar, pick places,
> set Start, Free until and Budget, then tap "Build my plan". Tap the trip between two stops to
> switch between walking, subway and driving.
> - To save a place: in TikTok or Instagram, tap Share on a post that names a place and choose
> Plandit. Or copy the post's link, then in Saved tap + and paste it. A video from Photos can be
> shared too; the app reads its text and speech on the phone and never uploads it.
> - To delete an account: Profile > Account > Delete account. It removes the account, saved places,
> plans and shared plan pages, and revokes the Sign in with Apple token.
> - On iPad, the app shows its iPhone layout as a panel over a full-screen map.
>
> 4. SHARED PLAN LINKS
> In Today, the share button can send the plan as a link. Anyone with the link can open a
> read-only web page of that plan (stops, times, optional prices and the user's note) without an
> account. The page isn't listed in the app or on the web, asks search engines not to index it,
> and has no comments or replies. The user can take it down at any time (share button > Stop
> sharing). There is no feed, public profile or messaging between users.
>
> 5. EXTERNAL SERVICES
> - Supabase: sign-up and sign-in (email, and Sign in with Apple through Supabase Auth), and the
> database for accounts, saved places and plans.
> - Render: hosts Plandit's API and the shared plan pages.
> - Sign in with Apple, including token revocation when an account is deleted.
> - Apple MapKit: the map, place search and directions.
> - TikTok and Instagram: when a user shares a post, our server reads that post's public page (and
> TikTok's public oEmbed endpoint) to get the caption and find the place.
> - Apple Vision and Speech, on the device only, for shared or attached videos.
> - Google Fonts: the shared plan page loads its font.
> Plandit uses no AI services, payment processors, ads or third-party analytics SDKs.
>
> 6. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
> Available in the United States only and made for New York City: transit costs use the NYC
> subway and bus fare ($2.90 a ride). Saving places, planning, the map, and walking and driving
> directions work the same in any region.
>
> 7. REGULATED INDUSTRY OR THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
> Not a regulated industry. Plandit shows the public caption and creator handle of a post the user
> chose to share, so they can see where a saved place came from. It doesn't download or re-host
> the post's video. Place and map data come from Apple Maps.
