- Notes (3529 of 4000 characters), as saved on November 11:

> 1. SCREEN RECORDING
> Attached below (Attachment: plandit-review.mp4). It was recorded on a physical iPhone with this
> build, then scaled down to fit the upload limit. It starts at launch and shows: creating an
> account with Sign in with Apple, saving a place from a TikTok link, deleting that account
> (Settings > Delete account, which asks Apple to confirm), signing in to the demo account,
> planning a day, the plan on the map, changing how to get between two stops, and sharing the
> plan.
> Plandit has no content that other users can see, so there is nothing to report or block.
> Nothing in the app is paid.
>
> 2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
> Plandit is a free day planner for college students in New York City. Students save TikToks and
> Reels of places they want to go, then rarely turn them into an actual day out. Plandit finds the
> place a shared post names and keeps it on a map. When the student has free time, it builds a
> plan for today from the places they pick: the order of stops, the time at each, how to get
> between them (walk, subway, bus or drive) and the cost per person against their budget. They
> can send the plan to a group chat; friends don't need the app.
>
> 3. HOW TO USE IT
> - Sign in with the demo account in Sign-In Information above ("Continue with email", then "Sign
> in"). You can also create an account with "Continue with email" (no email confirmation) or Sign
> in with Apple.
> - The demo account already has saved places. To plan a day: tap Plan (+) in the tab bar, pick
> places, set Start, Free until and Budget, then tap "Build my plan". The stops appear on the map
> and in Today. Tap the trip between two stops to switch between walking, subway and driving.
> - To save a place: in TikTok or Instagram, tap Share on a post that names a place and choose
> Plandit. Or copy the post's link and tap "Paste a link" in Saved. Plandit reads the post's
> public caption to find the place. If it isn't sure, it asks you to pick or to search for the
> place.
> - To delete an account: Settings > Delete account. It removes the account, saved places and
> plans, and revokes the Sign in with Apple token.
> - No sample files are needed.
> - On iPad, the app shows its iPhone layout as a panel over a full-screen map.
>
> 4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
> - Supabase: sign-up and sign-in (email, and Sign in with Apple through Supabase Auth), and the
> database for accounts, saved places and plans.
> - Render: hosts Plandit's API.
> - Sign in with Apple, including token revocation when an account is deleted.
> - Apple MapKit: the map, place search and directions.
> - TikTok and Instagram: when a user shares a post, our server reads that post's public page (and
> TikTok's public oEmbed endpoint) to get the caption and find the place.
> - Apple Vision and Speech, on the device only: if a user attaches a video of a place, the app
> reads its text and speech on the phone. The video is never uploaded.
> Plandit uses no AI services, payment processors, ads or third-party analytics SDKs.
>
> 5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
> Plandit is available in the United States only and is made for New York City: transit costs use
> the NYC subway and bus fare ($2.90 a ride). Everything else works the same in any region:
> saving places, planning, the map, and walking and driving directions.
>
> 6. REGULATED INDUSTRY OR THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
> Plandit is not in a regulated industry. It shows the public caption and creator handle of a post
> the user chose to share, so they can see where a saved place came from. It doesn't download or
> re-host the post's video. Place and map data come from Apple Maps.

## Sign in with Apple revocation

The "Plandit Sign in with Apple" key (primary App ID `com.plandit.app`) was created on November
10, 2026. Its Key ID and `.p8` are set in Render as `APPLE_SIGNIN_KEY_ID` and
`APPLE_SIGNIN_PRIVATE_KEY`.

Checked on November 11: two deletions of Apple accounts logged "Apple refresh_token revoked" in
the Render logs, at 2:19 PM and 2:38 PM (the second is the one in the App Review recording). The
server logs "No Apple authorization code … Apple tokens not revoked" for every deletion that
arrives without a code, which includes every email account, so that line alone is not a failure.
