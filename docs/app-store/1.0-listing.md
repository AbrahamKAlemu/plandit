## Text

**Subtitle** (30 max, from the website footer): Saved places. Real plans.

**Promotional text** (170 max): Share a TikTok or Reel to Plandit, pick a few saved spots, and get a
day that fits your time and budget. Then send it to the group chat.

**Description:**

> Your saves deserve to become a real day.
>
> Plandit turns the TikToks and Reels you've saved into a plan for today that fits your time and
> your budget, with every stop on a map.
>
> SAVE THE PLACES YOU ALREADY WANT
> • In TikTok or Instagram, tap Share, then choose Plandit. Or paste a link, including one a
> friend sent you.
> • Plandit finds the place in the post and adds it to your map, with its price and hours when it
> can find them. You can always set the price yourself.
> • Not sure which place a post means? Plandit asks you to pick instead of guessing.
>
> PLAN TODAY IN A FEW TAPS
> • Pick the saved places you want to get to, when you can start, when you need to be done, and
> your budget.
> • Plandit puts them in an order that makes sense, with the time, cost and travel between each
> stop, and leaves out places that are closed while you're free.
> • Walk, take the subway or drive, trip by trip, and watch the running total per person, subway
> fares included.
>
> ON THE DAY
> • Send the plan to your group chat: where to meet, what time, every stop and about what it costs
> per person. Your friends don't need the app.
> • Change a stop's time when plans move.
> • Get directions to what's next in Apple Maps.
>
> SIGN UP FREE
> Sign up with Apple or email to start. Your saved places and plans follow you to a new phone, and
> you can delete your account and everything in it any time in Settings.
>
> Plandit is free, with nothing to buy. Made for days out in New York City.

## App Review information

- Sign-in required: yes. **Demo account:** Abraham creates it in the app (email sign-up) and saves
  three or four places in it, because the planner needs saved places before it can build a day.
  Abraham types the email and password into App Store Connect.
- Contact: Abraham's name, phone and email, entered by Abraham.
- Notes:

> Plandit needs an account. Sign in with the demo account above, or create one with "Continue
> with email" (no email confirmation) or Sign in with Apple.
>
> Main flow: the demo account already has saved places. Tap + to plan a day, pick places, a start
> and end time and a budget, then tap "Add to today" to see the stops on the map.
>
> Saving a place: in TikTok or Instagram, tap Share on a post that names a place, then choose
> Plandit (or copy the post's link and use "Paste a link" in Saved). Plandit reads the post's
> public caption to find the place.
>
> Location is used only on the device to show the person on the map. Account deletion is in
> Settings → Account → Delete account.
